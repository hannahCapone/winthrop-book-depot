About Us!

Our Story

Welcome to Winthrop Book Depot & Cafe, a beloved town staple celebrating over five years of community support. Founded by the Martucci family, our cafe blends a warm atmosphere with friendly vibes, becoming a local favorite for both residents and students.

Susan Martucci (left) is the heart and soul of the cafe, ensuring every customer feels at home with her passion for books and meticulous attention to detail.

Suzanne Martucci (right) brings extensive experience in the coffee industry, dedicated to providing quality drinks and exceptional customer service. Her innovation keeps our menu fresh and exciting, featuring popular items like matcha tea, frozen chocolate drinks, and seasonal soups.

Our walls proudly display artwork from some of Winthrop's most talented artists, contributing to the unique charm of our cafe. Despite changes in the local school schedules, our loyal customers, including many students, continue to visit us regularly.

We are grateful for the unwavering support from our community and look forward to welcoming you to Winthrop Book Depot & Cafe, where every visit feels like coming home.
